Our History

Why ARSENAL Exists

ARSENAL is imagined as a place where military history is kept safe and made easy to understand. It would hold the uniforms, vehicles, and records of the past so that students, families, and researchers can learn from them.

Placeholder story: ARSENAL is a fictional museum created for design practice. Nothing on this page describes a real institution.

Our Mission

Keep the past safe. Share it with everyone.

Preserve

Collect and care for artifacts, uniforms, vehicles, and documents so they last for the generations that come after us.

Educate

Support schools and visitors with exhibitions and talks that explain the history behind each object, in clear and respectful language.
